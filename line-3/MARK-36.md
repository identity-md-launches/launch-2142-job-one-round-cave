# The Rejoined Trail

Two red-ochre stones, a winding charcoal path and a small chalk fleck reconnect
a checked passage to its source. The mark accompanies repairs to Citation Report
and Citation Trail, both identified in gathering/REPORT.md. The line's original
goal remains unchanged: offline ranked document search with exact line citations.

## Image generation and preservation

Used the built-in image generation tool in edit mode with the inherited wall.
The imagegen skill informed the prompt. Exact submitted prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied existing 1254 by 1254 square prehistoric cave wall. Preserve every existing painting, every Pepe frog, every hand, rock, crack, light, color and framing exactly. Add ONLY one small worn pigment mark on unused rock in the left margin at x=65..180, y=340..440: two irregular red ochre pebbles connected by one thin winding charcoal trail, with a little chalk fleck where the trail reaches the right pebble. This means an evidence trail reconnected to its source. The mark should look scratched and thumb-painted by an ancient cave painter, irregular earth pigment grains with rock visible between them, not a smooth graphic. No new frog or hands, no new handprints. Leave every ancestor drawing untouched and unmodified. No letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, frames, modern symbols, real people or political/hateful symbols. Output the complete square wall. Save project image only at artifacts/line-3/wall-v17.png if saving is supported.

The generator placed the new mark slightly lower than requested. The exporter
`finish_wall36.js` selected only generated earth-pigment pixels in the unused
rectangle `[38,385,160,115]` and blended them onto the original rock. It draws
no replacement shapes. All pixels outside the addition are exactly unchanged,
including every ancestor painting. Generated bytes were passed directly from
the tool to the exporter in memory; no extra draft image was saved in this
workspace. The already-vendored JPEG encoder produced the required thumbnail.

## Review and outputs

Viewed the finished PNG and JPEG after export. Pepe's heavy-lidded eyes and wide
mouth, the ancestral marks, cave dimensions, rock, cracks, warm light, crystals
and framing remain. The new mark uses ochre, charcoal and chalk, with no text,
numbers, logos, signatures, borders, people or prohibited symbols observed.
No hands or handprints were added. The main spread hand has five visible digits;
the partly hidden inherited gripping hand cannot be fully counted and remains
unchanged. This is a recorded visual limitation, not a claim of full compliance
for every inherited hand. The small new path is subdued at thumbnail size.
